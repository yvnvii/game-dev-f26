    Rules
    
      
      
        
    • On your turn, either drop a piece into one of 16 shafts, or twist one slice of the cube 90°.
      • 
        
    • A slice is a horizontal layer (turns around the vertical axis) or a vertical slab of shafts (A–D or 1–4). After a twist, gravity pulls every piece down its shaft.
      • 
        
    • Twist as often as you like, but a twist must change the board, and you can't undo the twist your opponent just made.
      • 
        
    • Four in a line wins, in any of the 76 lines. If a twist makes lines for both players, the twister wins; if only for the opponent, the opponent wins.
      • 
        
    • No winner after 100 turns: draw.
